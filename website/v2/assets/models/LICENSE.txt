Hand models: left.glb and right.glb
====================================

Source:  npm package @webxr-input-profiles/assets, version 1.0.20
         files dist/profiles/generic-hand/left.glb and right.glb
         https://www.npmjs.com/package/@webxr-input-profiles/assets
         https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles

The files are copied here unchanged, so the website does not need a CDN to show the hand.
The bones use the WebXR hand joint names (wrist, thumb-metacarpal, index-finger-phalanx-proximal, ...).
